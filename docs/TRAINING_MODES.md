not yet load payload bytes or execute a training mode. Corridor and exemplar
remain distinct declared surfaces: future P5 passes must execute corridor,
checkpoint, exemplar, checkpoint, without borrowing behavior from qrwkv-xla.

The Contract v4 profile declares sparse native-v3 resources, but P5.1 makes no
dense-target, NumPy-smoke, objective, batching, or loss claim. P5.2 owns typed
verified payload access; later checkpoints own materialization and execution.
